## Governance authority

The mainnet Registry grants `ADMIN_ROLE` to the timelock at
`0xF0911198Ef0a4b4234546fa5F50d6d1D45091774`. Its minimum delay is **172,800 seconds (48 hours)**
at the [dated deployment snapshot](/operations/current-deployments).

Governance can configure the connector weight, queued-exit minimum, feeds, supported assets,
strategy weights, performance-fee rate, treasury, and keeper parameters. It can register or remove
strategies, grant roles, unpause modules, and authorize UUPS upgrades. Controller 1.1.0
also permits ADMIN to call `withdrawFromStrategy(address,uint256)`.

A proposal in the DAO Safe is not yet a scheduled timelock operation. The delay starts when
the scheduling transaction executes. A Ready operation still needs an execution transaction.

## Emergency authority

`SECURITY_ROLE` permits pause and designated emergency recovery actions without the timelock.
It does not authorize UUPS upgrades or unpause. Specific configuration exceptions include
`StrategyManager.removeSupportedERC20()`, which either ADMIN or SECURITY may call to exclude
a supported token balance from NAV. This can restore accounting availability while reducing
reported backing; it does not recover the excluded asset. `Whitelist.removeSigner()` also
allows ADMIN or SECURITY to revoke an invite-voucher signer while the Whitelist is enabled.
Revocation is not permission to add signers or disable the Whitelist.

Pause controls are per contract. Registry pause gates its own guarded mutations, not
every peer contract. Strategy `emergencyExit()` requires that strategy to be paused and
allows ADMIN or SECURITY; a pause does not prove all assets were recovered.

A 48-hour review window does not guarantee a completed exit before execution. Queue timing,
liquidity, and pauses can make an exit take longer.

The dated snapshot confirms 018 executed and Controller 1.1.0 is active. Operations 019
and 020 are scheduled but not executed at that block. They register UniCL v2 and drain/remove
v1 respectively; 020 depends on 019's execution. Scheduling does not change the active basket.
