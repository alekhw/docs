These six terms are enough to follow the first-use guides. For implementation vocabulary,
use the complete [Reference glossary](/reference/glossary).

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **ETH** | Ethereum's native asset. You deposit ETH, redeem for ETH, and keep some in your wallet to pay transaction gas. |
| **EVE** | The protocol's share token. Its value can rise or fall with the managed portfolio. |
| **NAV** | Net asset value: the portfolio's ETH-valued assets after the protocol's accounting deductions. It is used to value EVE. |
| **Keeper** | Automation that submits authorized strategy and queue operations when conditions permit. It does not guarantee execution or settlement timing. |
| **Deposit** | Sending ETH to the protocol to mint EVE at the entry price. |
| **Redemption** | Exchanging EVE for ETH. The dated deployment snapshot queues new successful exits; after processing, you submit Claim separately. Immediate payment requires enough free AMM ETH. |
