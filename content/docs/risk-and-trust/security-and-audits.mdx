## Review coverage

Public development history contains review-driven fixes and tests. Those are useful evidence
of engineering work, but do not substitute for an independent audit report.
This documentation audit did not establish whether an engagement exists outside the reviewed
repositories. It does not certify the deployed protocol or the proposed v2 migration.

When a report is published, link its artifact, auditor, exact code revision, covered contracts,
and remediation status. Coverage of v1 does not automatically cover a different v2 build.

## Immutable core and configurable dependencies

EVE, AMM, Registry, and individual UniCL strategy instances have fixed deployed bytecode.
Controller, StrategyManager, ExitQueue, Oracle, and Converter are upgradeable.
The timelock held ADMIN and had a 48-hour delay at the
[dated deployment snapshot](/operations/current-deployments).

Fixed AMM code is not a guarantee that its inputs remain unchanged: Registry peers, NAV
logic, oracle feeds, connector weight, and other settings can change through authorized paths.
Performance-fee configuration and treasury EVE minting live in StrategyManager.

## Emergency authority

ADMIN or SECURITY can pause designated modules and use designated emergency paths.
SECURITY also has the specific ability to remove supported ERC-20s from StrategyManager
accounting and revoke Whitelist signers while that gate is enabled. It cannot add Whitelist
signers, disable that gate, unpause, or authorize upgrades.

Pauses are per contract. Registry pause gates only its own guarded mutations; it does not
pause every peer. A paused AMM blocks normal entry/exit, and other module pauses block
their relevant operations. Strategy `emergencyExit()` permits ADMIN or SECURITY and requires
the strategy to be paused.
They do not block AMM's claim or eligible cancellation paths.
The timelock is a review window. It does not guarantee that an exit will settle before a change.

## Fail-closed valuation

A registered strategy NAV revert propagates into total NAV.
An invalid feed for a nonzero supported-token balance can also block valuation.
Normal mints, burns, and batch pricing may then fail rather than trade at an unverifiable NAV.

This does not mean all protocol functions stop. Claims and eligible cancellations do not
require fresh NAV. A paused strategy can still report NAV if its view remains functional.

Ordinary strategy removal requires NAV at most 10 wei and a successful NAV read.
ADMIN's force-removal path can remove a strategy despite a failed read or residual assets.
It does not recover those assets, and their value is no longer included in the registered basket.

## Actual price and execution protections

AMM checks the user's mint minimum, maximum EVE input for both immediate and queued exits,
and queued price tolerance.
Queued settlement outside tolerance returns EVE rather than crediting ETH.
The queue bounds live priced batches to 25.

There is **no deployed AMM 5% rolling-price checkpoint guard or checkpoint-reset action**.
UniCL pool/TWAP checks are strategy-level controls. They do not protect the AMM as a whole against NAV changes.

## Pull-based claims

Processing credits ETH to AMM's claimable balance, and reserves it in `lockedForClaims`.
The user calls `claim()` separately. This avoids pushing ETH to every recipient during
batch processing, but claim submission still depends on the user's wallet and gas.

## Automation boundary

Mimic functions call allowlisted keeper executors. Execution values are re-derived on-chain;
off-chain payloads do not supply authoritative NAV or settlement amounts.
Availability still depends on tasks, funding, RPC, gas policies, feeds, and contract eligibility.
