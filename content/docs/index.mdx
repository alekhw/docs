  Everstrat is currently deployed on **Arbitrum Sepolia** (chain id **421614**). Do not send real funds.
  All activity uses testnet ETH with no real-money value.
</Callout>

Everstrat lets you deposit ETH to receive EVE tokens, which represent your share of managed on-chain
strategies. Strategies are executed by a permissioned controller; you can exit at any time through
immediate redemption or the queued exit model. This site documents the product, protocol, and
operational details for users, integrators, and contributors.
