## Understand the quote

Deposits mint EVE at the **entry (premium) price**. When the connector weight is below one,
the entry price is higher than the base price used for redemption. An immediate round trip
can therefore return less ETH, even before gas. The gap is not an entry fee paid to a fee recipient.

Review [Fees & costs](/using-everstrat/fees-and-costs). Exact formulas, the connector weight,
and a worked example live in [Pricing & the bonding curve](/how-everstrat-works/pricing-and-bonding-curve).
[EVE & NAV](/how-everstrat-works/eve-and-nav) explains the accounting behind the quote.

## Slippage protection

The estimated EVE output can change before confirmation. **Max slippage** sets a minimum
acceptable EVE output (`minTokensToMint`). Leave it on **Auto** or enter a manual percentage.
If the confirmed transaction cannot meet that minimum, it reverts rather than minting fewer
tokens. A reverted transaction can still consume gas.

## Deposit transaction states

- **Before submission:** check invite access, Ethereum mainnet, your ETH amount, and gas reserve.
  The first mint for a wallet/network requires the app's risk acknowledgment. Read the
  [Risk Disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure) and continue only if both statements are true.
- **Wallet confirmation:** verify the AMM destination, ETH amount, and gas estimate. Native ETH
  minting does not require an ERC-20 EVE approval.
- **Submitted:** a transaction hash means the transaction was sent. Wait for its receipt to confirm success.
- **Confirmed:** EVE is minted and your wallet balance updates. Review the deposit on **Portfolio**.
  Indexed activity may take longer to update than current RPC balances.
- **Cancelled or failed:** declining a wallet prompt does not mint EVE. A reverted transaction does
  not complete the deposit; check the failure reason before resubmitting.

## Common blockers

- **Invite access unavailable:** finish the app's access flow; if required signing access is
  unavailable, wait rather than sending ETH directly to a contract.
- **Insufficient ETH:** reduce the deposit and retain enough ETH for gas.
- **AMM paused or valuation unavailable:** minting cannot proceed until the relevant condition
  is resolved. A quote shown earlier is not evidence that a new transaction can succeed.
- **Quote moved beyond slippage:** refresh and review the new output before deciding whether to retry.
- **First deposit into a new deployment:** the one-time bootstrap has additional conditions.
  See [Bootstrap rules](/how-everstrat-works/pricing-and-bonding-curve#bootstrap-rules).
  These rules apply once, when a deployment initializes supply.
