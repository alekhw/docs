When you exit Everstrat you burn EVE tokens and receive ETH at the current **base price**
(`NAV / live EVE supply`, excluding in-window priced queued EVE). There are two exit paths depending on how much free ETH the AMM is holding at
that moment.

<DocsDiagram name="exit" />

## Two exit paths

<Tabs items={["Immediate exit", "Queued exit"]}>
  <Tab>
    If the AMM's free ETH balance is large enough to cover your redemption, you receive ETH in the
    **same transaction**. No waiting. No extra steps.

    Immediate exits have **no minimum size**.

    Slippage protection on this path is `maxTokensToBurn` — a hard cap on how much EVE can be burned
    for the ETH you requested. If the price moves against you beyond that cap, the transaction reverts.
    (`priceTolerance` plays no role in an immediate exit.)
  </Tab>
  <Tab>
    If the AMM does not have enough free liquidity, your exit request joins a **batch queue**. Your EVE
    is escrowed and you wait for the keeper to settle the batch. See the lifecycle below.

    Queued exits must be worth at least **0.001 ETH** by default (`DEFAULT_MIN_BATCH_EXIT_ETH`). This
    minimum can be raised by governance up to a maximum of **0.05 ETH**
    (`MIN_BATCH_EXIT_ETH_UPPER_BOUND`).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Queued exit lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Submit your exit request.** You call the exit function with the ETH amount you want back
    (`requestedETH`), a cap on the EVE you are willing to burn (`maxTokensToBurn`), and a
    `priceTolerance`. Your EVE tokens are escrowed — they leave your wallet and are held by the
    protocol until the batch is settled.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Keeper prices the batch.** A keeper bot monitors the queue. When conditions are met, it prices
    the batch at `AMM.eveBasePriceInETH()`, locking in one price for all requests in that batch.
    Each request is later checked against its own request-time price and chosen tolerance.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Keeper processes the batch.** The keeper sends ETH to cover the batch and marks each request as
    fulfilled.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **ETH is credited to your claimable balance.** After processing, the ETH you are owed is recorded
    in `claimableBalances[yourAddress]`. It is not sent to you automatically (pull-over-push pattern).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Call Claim to withdraw your ETH.** You call the `claim()` function to pull your ETH out of the
    protocol and into your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Slippage protection (`priceTolerance`)

Price can fall between the moment you submit your exit request and the moment the keeper prices the
batch. `priceTolerance` sets your floor: if the final batch price has dropped by more than your
tolerance relative to the price at the time of your request, your request is **closed for slippage** —
your EVE is returned to you and you receive no ETH. You can resubmit at any time.

`priceTolerance` applies only to queued exits; immediate exits are protected by `maxTokensToBurn`
instead (see above).

## Escape hatch — cancelling a redemption

There are two situations where you can cancel a pending exit and get your escrowed EVE back:

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Before the batch is priced">
    Any time before the keeper has priced a batch, you can call `cancelRedemption(batchId)` to cancel
    your request and retrieve your EVE. There is no time constraint for this — you can cancel an
    unpriced batch immediately after submitting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="More than 3 days after pricing, still unprocessed">
    Once a keeper has priced a batch (`batch.pricedAt` is set), a **3-day clock**
    (`MAX_BATCH_PROCESSING_TIME`) starts. When **more than** 3 days have passed and your request is
    still unprocessed, you can call `cancelRedemption(batchId)` to reclaim your escrowed EVE.
    At exactly 3 days, cancellation remains blocked. Once processed, the request cannot be
    cancelled; withdraw its credited ETH with `claim()`.

    Note: the 3-day clock starts from when the batch is **priced**, not from when you submitted your
    exit request.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Step-by-step exit

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Open the app and connect your wallet** on Ethereum mainnet.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Enter the EVE amount** you want to burn. The app shows your estimated ETH at the current base
    price.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Set your price tolerance.** This bounds how much the price can fall before your queued request
    is automatically cancelled and your EVE returned.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Confirm the transaction.** If the AMM has enough free ETH, you receive ETH immediately.
    Otherwise, your request enters the queue.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **If queued, wait for the keeper to settle, then Claim.** Check the app for the status of your
    batch. Once it is processed, call Claim to withdraw your ETH.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a guided walkthrough of your first exit, see [Your first exit](/get-started/burn-and-redeem).
