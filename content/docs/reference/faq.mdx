<Callout type="warn" title="Testnet only">
  Everstrat is on **Sepolia testnet (chain id 11155111)**. Tokens have no monetary value. Do not
  send real funds.
</Callout>

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Is Everstrat live on mainnet?">
    No. Everstrat is deployed on **Sepolia testnet** (chain id 11155111). There is no mainnet
    deployment. EVE tokens and any ETH you interact with on this testnet have no monetary value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is EVE and what backs it?">
    EVE is a token that represents your share of the protocol's net asset value (NAV). When you deposit
    ETH, the AMM mints EVE proportional to your share of the total NAV. When you exit, you burn EVE and
    receive ETH back at the current NAV per EVE (the base price). The NAV is made up of ETH held across
    the AMM, Controller, StrategyManager, and any deployed yield strategies. See
    [EVE and NAV](/how-everstrat-works/eve-and-nav) for the full formula.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I deposit ETH and receive EVE?">
    Connect your wallet on Sepolia, enter an ETH amount, and confirm the transaction. The AMM
    calculates how much EVE to mint using the premium price (which is higher than the redemption base
    price). You can set a `minTokensToMint` floor to protect against slippage. See
    [Depositing & minting](/using-everstrat/depositing-and-minting) for a step-by-step walkthrough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I exit and get my ETH back?">
    Go to the exit screen, enter the amount of EVE to burn, and submit. If the AMM has enough free ETH,
    your exit settles immediately. If not, it enters the exit queue and you claim ETH after the batch is
    processed. See [Exiting & redeeming](/using-everstrat/exiting-and-redeeming) for the full flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my exit get queued, and how long does it take?">
    Your exit is queued when the AMM does not have enough free ETH to settle it immediately — for
    example, because most of the NAV is deployed in a yield strategy. Once queued, a keeper prices the
    batch (at the AMM's base price, not an oracle price) and processes it. After processing, ETH is
    credited to your claimable balance and you must call `claim()` to withdraw it (pull-over-push). If
    the keeper has not processed the batch within **3 days** of pricing, you can invoke the escape hatch
    (`cancelRedemption`) to cancel the request and recover your EVE.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel a queued exit?">
    Yes, in two situations:

    - **Before the batch is priced:** you can cancel at any time with no restrictions.
    - **After the batch is priced:** you can cancel only after **3 days have passed** since the batch
      was priced (the escape hatch). Inside that window, the cancel call will revert.

    If the settled price has dropped more than your `priceTolerance` below the price at request time,
    the protocol automatically closes your request and returns your EVE — you do not need to take
    action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What fees does Everstrat charge?">
    There is **no protocol fee** on deposit or exit — your full ETH is forwarded on entry, and the full
    ETH value of your EVE is redeemed on exit.

    Costs you do pay:

    - **Entry premium** — you deposit at the premium price (higher than base price). The difference goes
      to existing EVE holders, not the protocol. See [Fees & costs](/using-everstrat/fees-and-costs).
    - **Performance fee** — up to **20%** of NAV growth above the strategy's high-water mark, paid to
      the DAO treasury. This reduces NAV growth flowing to you rather than appearing as a direct charge.
    - **Gas** — standard Sepolia network gas on every on-chain action, including deposit, exit
      submission, and claim.
    - **Slippage** — price can move between submission and settlement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I lose money?">
    Yes. NAV can fall if the yield strategy loses value or if the protocol encounters an issue. You
    receive ETH proportional to NAV at the time you exit — if NAV has fallen since you deposited, you
    receive less ETH than you put in. Because this is testnet, no real money is at risk right now, but
    the mechanism is real. See [Key risks](/risk-and-trust/key-risks) for a full breakdown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Has Everstrat been audited?">
    No audit is documented in this repository. The protocol is on Sepolia testnet and should
    be treated as unaudited. Do not treat testnet deployment as a signal of production readiness.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What network are the contracts on?">
    All contracts are on **Sepolia** (chain id 11155111). See
    [Contract addresses](/reference/contract-addresses) for the full list of deployed addresses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My wallet is connected but transactions are not starting">
    Check that your wallet is switched to **Sepolia** (chain id 11155111). Everstrat does not
    support any other network. If you see a network mismatch prompt, approve the switch. If transactions
    still do not initiate, refresh the page and reconnect your wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The AMM shows a paused or excessive-deviation error">
    The AMM can be paused by the ADMIN_ROLE or SECURITY_ROLE in an emergency, or transactions can revert
    with `AMMExcessiveDeviation` if the EVE (base) price has moved more than the allowed deviation
    (default 5%) from its checkpoint. If you see this error, wait for the team to reset the price
    checkpoint (an ADMIN_ROLE or SECURITY_ROLE action) before retrying. You cannot work around this
    yourself.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My queued exit is still pending after several days">
    If more than 3 days have passed since your batch was priced, you can use the escape hatch to cancel
    the request and recover your EVE. If the batch has not been priced yet, you can cancel at any time.
    See the question above on cancelling a queued exit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The activity feed is lagging behind my transaction">
    The activity history uses the GoldSky subgraph, which indexes on-chain events with a short delay.
    Your transaction may have settled on-chain before the subgraph reflects it. Wait a minute and
    refresh — if it still does not appear, check the transaction hash directly on Etherscan.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
