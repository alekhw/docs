## Purpose

`Controller` is the operational coordinator. It receives ETH from AMM, interacts with StrategyManager,
and manages priced queue processing through keeper flows.

## Main Powers

- distribute ETH to strategies
- withdraw or queue withdrawals from strategies
- rebalance unhealthy strategies
- price exit batches
- process queued redemption requests

## Role Model

| Role | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ADMIN_ROLE` | upgrade authorization and emergency functions |
| `KEEPER_ROLE` | routine strategy and queue operations |

## Important Integration Edge

Controller does not directly replace AMM or ExitQueue. It receives operational privileges on those
contracts through `CONTROLLER_ROLE`.
