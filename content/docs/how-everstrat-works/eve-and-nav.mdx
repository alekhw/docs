## EVE is a proportional claim

When you deposit ETH into Everstrat, you receive **EVE** — an ERC-20 token that represents your
fractional share of the protocol's total **net asset value (NAV)**, measured in ETH.

Your share of the protocol is simple:

```text
live supply = total EVE supply - in-window priced queued EVE
your active share = your EVE balance / live supply
```

As the protocol's strategies earn (or lose) value, NAV rises or falls, and each EVE token is worth
correspondingly more or less ETH. There is no promised return. EVE is not pegged to anything.

## What counts as NAV

StrategyManager's `totalNAVInETH()` starts with the following gross assets, then deducts
the live priced queued liability described below:

| Component | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy `navInETH()` calls | Each registered strategy's reported ETH value — for UniCLStrat this includes idle ETH and WETH, the LP position value, uncollected trading fees, and paired-token inventory (the paired token is valued via the Oracle). |
| StrategyManager balance | Any ETH sitting on the StrategyManager contract itself, waiting to be deployed. |
| Controller balance | Any ETH held by the Controller — received from the AMM but not yet forwarded to strategies. |
| AMM free balance | ETH in the AMM contract that is not reserved for pending claims: `address(AMM).balance − lockedForClaims`. |
| Supported-ERC20 balances on the StrategyManager | Supported tokens held by the manager, valued through `Oracle.convert`; for example, paired-token inventory received through emergency recovery. A zero balance contributes no value. |

From that asset total, it subtracts the ETH liability of priced, unprocessed requests whose batches
are still inside the 3-day processing window (`liveRedemptionOffsets()`). Unpriced requests remain
equity; their escrowed EVE is not deducted from NAV. The priced liability expires with the processing
window, without requiring a reset transaction. AMM pricing also excludes in-window priced redemption
shares from its live supply denominator.

`lockedForClaims` tracks ETH already owed to users who have had their queued exit processed but have
not yet called `claim()`. That ETH is excluded from NAV because it is no longer the protocol's to
deploy.

The full formula is:

```text
NAV = strategy NAVs + StrategyManager ETH + Controller ETH + AMM free ETH
      + supported ERC-20 value held by StrategyManager
      - in-window priced redemption liabilities
base price = NAV / live supply
```

## Fail-closed NAV

The NAV calculation is **fail-closed**: if any strategy's `navInETH()` call reverts — for example
because a required oracle or pool observation is unavailable, or the strategy is broken — then `totalNAVInETH()` reverts
too. When NAV cannot be computed, the AMM cannot price anything, so `enter()`, `exit()`, and batch
pricing all freeze until the problem is resolved.

<Callout type="info">
  This is a deliberate safety property. Allowing enters and exits to proceed against a stale or missing
  NAV figure would let users trade at a price that does not reflect reality, which could drain value
  from the remaining holders. The protocol prefers a temporary freeze over mispricing.
</Callout>

A paused UniCL strategy can still report NAV. Pausing alone does not imply a failed NAV read.
Non-zero supported ERC-20 balances on StrategyManager also require valid oracle prices.

Governance can recover funds or use `StrategyManager.forceRemoveStrategy()` to deregister a
strategy whose NAV reverts. Normal `removeStrategy()` requires its reported NAV to be at most
10 wei and propagates a failed NAV read. Forced removal drops any remaining strategy assets out
of protocol NAV; it does not automatically recover them.

## Why this matters to you

- **NAV changes** can come from deposits, exits, fees, asset prices, donations, or recovery actions.
  NAV alone is not a return measure. Compare the base price per EVE, and account for fee dilution
  and queued liabilities.
- **Unavailable NAV** can block normal entry and exit until valuation recovers or governance acts.
  Claims of already credited ETH and eligible queued cancellations do not require fresh NAV.
