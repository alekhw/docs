<Callout type="warn" title="Testing phase — treat as a test protocol">
  Everstrat is deployed on **Ethereum mainnet** (chain id 1), but it is in a **testing phase** and
  should be treated as a **test protocol**. Entry is invite-gated, the software is experimental and
  unaudited, and deposits are real ETH at real risk. Do not deposit more than you can afford to
  lose entirely.
</Callout>

You deposit ETH and receive **EVE** — a token representing your share of a managed on-chain strategy
portfolio. A keeper deploys the pooled ETH into yield strategies, and EVE's value tracks the
portfolio's net asset value (NAV). You can exit any time by burning EVE for ETH.

## What you'll do

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Connect a wallet on Ethereum mainnet.** The app only works on mainnet — it will
    prompt you to switch if you're on another network. Entry requires a whitelist invite while
    the invite period is active.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Deposit ETH to mint EVE.** Enter an amount, review your estimated EVE, and confirm. EVE's value
    is tied to the portfolio NAV.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Burn EVE to redeem ETH.** When you're ready to exit, burn your EVE. Depending on available
    liquidity, you'll receive ETH immediately or after a short queued process.
  </Step>
</Steps>
