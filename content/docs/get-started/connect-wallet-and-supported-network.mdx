---
title: Connect your wallet
description: Connect a compatible wallet, switch to Ethereum, and redeem wallet-linked invite access.
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Open the official app and prepare your invite.** During the invite period, `/mint`,
    `/portfolio`, and `/metrics` require an access session; without one they redirect to `/`.
    Use your invitation link or code. A wallet connection alone does not grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Connect your wallet.** Select **Connect Wallet** and choose a detected browser wallet,
    or WalletConnect if that option is configured. Approve the connection in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Switch to Ethereum mainnet (chain ID 1).** If connected on another network, select the
    app's **Wrong network** button and approve the switch in your wallet. Continue once the
    app no longer shows **Wrong network**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Accept the invite with this wallet.** Enter your code if needed, select **Accept invite**,
    and review and sign the access message. This signature is not a deposit transaction and costs
    no gas. The invite is linked to the wallet that redeems it; use that wallet for subsequent access.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Keep ETH available for deposits and gas.** Every transaction uses real mainnet ETH.
    Review [Before you start](/get-started/overview) before minting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Testing phase — treat as a test protocol">
  Everstrat is deployed on **Ethereum mainnet** and entry is **invite-gated** while the invite
  period is active. The software is experimental and unaudited. Deposits are real ETH at real
  risk — never deposit more than you can afford to lose entirely, and only interact with the
  addresses listed in [Contract addresses](/reference/contract-addresses).
</Callout>
