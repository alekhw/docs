---
title: Connect your wallet
description: How to connect a compatible wallet, add Sepolia, and get testnet ETH before using the app.
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Use a supported wallet.** The app connects wallets through RainbowKit and wagmi. Any wallet
    supported by that setup — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, WalletConnect-compatible wallets, and others —
    will work.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Add and switch to Sepolia (chain id 11155111).** The app only supports this single
    testnet network. If you connect on a different chain, the app will prompt you to switch
    automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Get testnet ETH.** You need Sepolia ETH to deposit. Search for an Sepolia
    faucet and request test funds — they're free and have no real-world value.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Click Connect Wallet** in the top-right corner of the app. Select your wallet from the modal,
    approve the connection, and you're ready to go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Testnet only">
  Everstrat runs on **Sepolia testnet only**. Never send mainnet ETH or any real-value token
  to the app or its contracts. All tokens on testnet have no monetary value.
</Callout>
