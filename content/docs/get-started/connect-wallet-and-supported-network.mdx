---
title: Connect your wallet
description: How to connect a compatible wallet and switch to Ethereum mainnet before using the app.
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Use a supported wallet.** The app connects wallets through RainbowKit and wagmi. Any wallet
    supported by that setup — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, WalletConnect-compatible wallets, and others —
    will work.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Switch to Ethereum mainnet (chain id 1).** The app only supports mainnet. If you connect on a
    different chain, the app will prompt you to switch automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Have real ETH available.** Deposits are real mainnet ETH. Everstrat is in a testing phase —
    treat it as a test protocol and only deposit what you can afford to lose entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Click Connect Wallet** in the top-right corner of the app. Select your wallet from the modal,
    approve the connection, and you're ready to go.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Testing phase — treat as a test protocol">
  Everstrat is deployed on **Ethereum mainnet** and entry is **invite-gated** while the invite
  period is active. The software is experimental and unaudited. Deposits are real ETH at real
  risk — never deposit more than you can afford to lose entirely, and only interact with the
  addresses listed in [Contract addresses](/reference/contract-addresses).
</Callout>
