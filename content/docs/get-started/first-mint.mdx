---
title: Your first deposit
description: Review an ETH deposit, acknowledge risks, and mint EVE on Ethereum mainnet.
---

Complete [Before you start](/get-started/overview) and
[Connect your wallet](/get-started/connect-wallet-and-supported-network) first.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Select Mint mode.** If the form is in Burn mode, use the **Mint** text button beneath
    the main action.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Enter an ETH amount.** Type an amount or use the balance slider. Leave ETH in your wallet
    for gas and future transactions.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Review the estimated EVE and max slippage.** The quote uses the current entry price.
    Leave slippage on **Auto** or enter a manual percentage. Your minimum acceptable EVE output
    protects the transaction if the quote changes before confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Click Mint and read “Before you mint.”** On your first mint for this wallet and network,
    tick both checkboxes: accept the Risk Disclosure and total-loss risk, and confirm you are
    not a citizen or resident of the United States and not located in, or a citizen or resident of,
    another jurisdiction where access is restricted or prohibited by law or sanctions.
    Continue only if both statements are true. Select
    **I accept — continue to mint** to proceed, or **Cancel** to stop. This acknowledgment is
    remembered in your browser for that wallet and network; it may appear again in another
    browser or after stored data is cleared.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Confirm the mint transaction in your wallet.** Check the network, destination, ETH amount,
    and gas estimate. The app shows the transaction's progress.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Wait for confirmation.** A confirmed mint updates your EVE balance. Review the result
    on **Portfolio** and activity in the wallet account dialog, opened from the header's
    connected-wallet button. A quote or wallet prompt alone is not a completed deposit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the action is blocked or fails, do not repeatedly approve prompts without reviewing the error.
See [Depositing & minting](/using-everstrat/depositing-and-minting) for pricing expectations,
transaction conditions, and the one-time bootstrap rule.

Next: [Your first exit](/get-started/burn-and-redeem). Keep the
[Risk Disclosure](/legal/risk-disclosure) and [Fees & costs](/using-everstrat/fees-and-costs)
in mind before submitting another transaction.
