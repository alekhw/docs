---
title: Your first deposit
description: How to deposit ETH and mint EVE on Sepolia, step by step.
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Make sure the toggle is set to Mint.** The app has a Mint/Burn toggle at the top of the form.
    Select **Mint** (shown in green). This puts you in deposit mode.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Enter an ETH amount.** Type a value into the amount field, or use the percentage slider
    (**0 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100%**) to fill based on your wallet balance. The balance pill shows your
    current ETH balance for reference.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Review the estimated EVE you'll receive.** The app shows how much EVE you'll get based on the
    current portfolio NAV and the entry (premium) price. You won't see the exact final amount until
    the transaction is mined, which is why slippage matters.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Set your slippage.** This controls `minTokensToMint` — the fewest EVE you'll accept. If the
    price moves against you before the transaction confirms, it reverts instead of giving you less EVE
    than expected. You can leave it on **Auto** or enter a manual basis-point value.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Click Mint and approve in your wallet.** Confirm the transaction in your wallet when prompted.
    The app will display a transaction status dialog showing progress.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Wait for confirmation.** Once the transaction is confirmed, your EVE balance updates and the
    deposit is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="More about depositing">
  There's more to know about depositing: the first-ever deposit into the protocol (bootstrap) has a
  minimum size requirement, and entry always uses a premium price above NAV. See
  [Depositing and minting](/using-everstrat/depositing-and-minting) for a worked example and full
  explanation.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="AMM paused">
  If you see **"AMM is currently paused"**, minting and burning are temporarily unavailable — check
  back later. Claiming ETH you are already owed and the escape-hatch cancel keep working while paused,
  so a pause never locks up funds that have been credited to you.
</Callout>
