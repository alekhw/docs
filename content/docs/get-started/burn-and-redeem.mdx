Use Ethereum mainnet and keep ETH in your wallet for gas. Review
[Before you start](/get-started/overview) and [Fees & costs](/using-everstrat/fees-and-costs).

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Select Burn mode.** Use the **Burn** text button beneath the main action.
    The input is labelled **You will burn**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Enter an EVE amount.** Type an amount or use the slider to choose part of your balance.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Review the ETH estimate and max slippage.** Leave slippage on **Auto** or choose a manual
    percentage. Read any minimum-size or pending-request warning before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Approve EVE if requested.** If your allowance is insufficient, the button reads
    **Approve EVE**. Verify the AMM spender and allowance, then confirm this approval transaction
    in your wallet and wait for confirmation. Approval lets the AMM transfer EVE; it does not
    submit your exit or send ETH back. The app skips this step if allowance is already sufficient.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Click Burn and confirm the exit transaction.** After approval, check the updated quote
    and confirm **Burn** separately in your wallet. Wait for the transaction result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

At the [dated snapshot](/operations/current-deployments#exit-liquidity-at-this-block), AMM free ETH
is zero and routine float top-up is disabled, so new successful exits queue.

<Tabs items={["Queued exit (current snapshot)", "Immediate exit (conditional)"]}>
  <Tab>
    AMM holds your EVE while the request waits in the exit queue. Submission does not mean
    ETH has been paid.

    1. Review your request on **Portfolio** while keepers price, fund, and process it.
    2. After successful processing, ETH is credited to your claimable balance.
    3. Select **Claim** and confirm the separate transaction in your wallet to withdraw that ETH.

    Settlement is not guaranteed. If closed for slippage, the request returns EVE instead of ETH.
    Strictly more than three days after pricing, an unprocessed request cannot be paid; cancel
    it to recover EVE. Processed requests must be claimed, and already credited ETH remains claimable.
    See [Exiting & redeeming](/using-everstrat/exiting-and-redeeming) for conditions.
  </Tab>
  <Tab>
    Code supports this branch only if enough unreserved AMM ETH becomes available. It is not
    funded at the dated snapshot. If available and the exit succeeds, EVE is burned and ETH
    is transferred to your wallet in the same transaction. No separate Claim is needed.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The detailed [exit guide](/using-everstrat/exiting-and-redeeming) covers minimum amounts,
pending-request limits, pauses, and transaction states. Exact pricing and queue rules live in
[How Everstrat Works](/how-everstrat-works/redemption-queue).
